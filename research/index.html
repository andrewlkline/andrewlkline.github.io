ResearchPeskiza

Documenting the languages of Timor-Leste, with a focus on Kairui-Midiki and its place within the Kawaimina and Timor-Babar language groups. Halo dokumentasaun lian materna Timor-Leste nian, foka liu ba lian Kairui-Midiki ho nia pozisaun iha grupu “Kawaimina” no Timor-Babar.

Current Research FocusPeskiza

My research interests center on the documentation of the languages of Timor-Leste, with a primary focus on the Kairui-Midiki language [ISO 639-3: krd]. Kairui-Midiki is spoken in the Venilale Administrative Post, where I lived and worked for two years during my Peace Corps service. Ha'u-nia peskiza foka ba dokumentasaun lian inan sira iha Timor-Leste, liuliu lian Midiki (Kairui). Midiki lian lokál ida ne'ebé mak lokaliza iha Munisípiu Baucau, Postu Administrativu Venilale. Durante tinan rua nia laran, ha'u hela no servisu iha Venilale hanesan voluntáriu husi Korpu da Pás. Se karik Ita-Bo'ot ema Midiki ka lian lokál sira seluk, no hakarak hola parte iha peskiza ka dokumentasaun ne'e, bele kontaktu ha'u liuhusi ha'u-nia email klineal@hawaii.edu. Obrigadu waru-waru!

I am particularly interested in the historical dynamics in the region. Specifically, I am investigating the Kawaimina languages' historical development and classification within the Timor-Babar branch. Additionally, the long-term contact between Kairui-Midiki — an Austronesian language — and the non-Austronesian (Papuan) languages spoken in Venilale and eastern Timor is of interest. Ha'u mós iha interese atu estuda liu tan kontaktu istóriku entre lian lokál sira iha Timor. Liuliu foka ba interasaun entre lian Midiki (lian Austronesia nian) ho lian sira bolu lian Papua nian (hanesan Makasae, Makalero, Fataluku, Bunaq).
