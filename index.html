Current Research FocusPeskiza

My research interests center on the documentation of the languages of Timor-Leste, with a primary focus on the Kairui-Midiki language [ISO 639-3: krd]. Kairui-Midiki is spoken in the Venilale Administrative Post, where I lived and worked for two years during my Peace Corps service. Ha'u-nia peskiza foka ba dokumentasaun lian inan sira iha Timor-Leste, liuliu lian Midiki (Kairui). Midiki lian lokál ida ne'ebé mak lokaliza iha Munisípiu Baucau, Postu Administrativu Venilale. Durante tinan rua nia laran, ha'u hela no servisu iha Venilale hanesan voluntáriu husi Korpu da Pás. Se karik Ita-Bo'ot ema Midiki ka lian lokál sira seluk, no hakarak hola parte iha peskiza ka dokumentasaun ne'e, bele kontaktu ha'u liuhusi ha'u-nia email klineal@hawaii.edu. Obrigadu waru-waru!
