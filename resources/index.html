Makasae TAP

MA Dissertation

Huber, Juliette (2008). First Steps Towards a Grammar of Makasae: A Language of East Timor. Sketch grammar based on the variety spoken in the subdistrict of Ossu, Viqueque district (southern Makasae).

Dissertation

Correia, Aderito (2011). Describing Makasae: A Trans-New Guinea Language of East Timor. Western Sydney University. Based on the author’s native variety spoken in the subdistrict of Laga, Baucau district. Includes a Makasae–English, English–Makasae word list.

Book Chapter

Huber, Juliette (2017). “Makalero and Makasae.” In The Papuan Languages of Timor, Alor and Pantar, vol. 2, ed. Antoinette Schapper, 267–351.

Honours Thesis

Brotherson, Anna (2003). A Spatial Odyssey: Referring to Space in Makasai. Australian National University. Difficult to find digitally.

Dissertation

Fogaça, Jessé Silveira (2016). Aspectos Gramaticais da Língua Makasae de Timor-Leste: Fonologia, Morfologia e Sintaxe. Universidade de Brasília. Description of Makasae phonology, morphology, and syntax, in Portuguese.

Makalero TAP

Dissertation

Huber, Juliette (2011). A Grammar of Makalero: A Papuan Language of East Timor. Universiteit Leiden. Descriptive grammar as spoken in the subdistrict of Iliomar, Lautém district. Includes a lengthy Makalero–English, English–Makalero word list.

Book Chapter

Huber, Juliette (2017). “Makalero and Makasae.” In The Papuan Languages of Timor, Alor and Pantar, vol. 2, ed. Antoinette Schapper, 267–351.

Dictionary

Pinto, Thomas Mariano (2012). Vocabulariu: Makalero–Tetun–Portugues–Ingles. Timor Lorosa’e–Nippon Culture Center. Four-language dictionary. Difficult to find digitally.
