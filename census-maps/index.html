2022 Census Mother-Tongue Maps

Interactive suco-level maps of home-language distribution from the 2022 Timor-Leste Population and Housing Census, with a focus on the Kairui-Midiki (Kawaimina) homeland in Baucau, Manatuto, and Viqueque. Updates Williams-van Klinken & Williams (2015) with the 2022 census and original fieldwork.

A methodological note: the 2022 census asked mother tongue as a checklist of 33 yes/no items, not a single-choice question like 2010 and 2015. People could (and did) check more than one language, so 2022 totals do not sum to the population and are not directly comparable to earlier census years. Tetun Prasa in particular jumps from 30.5% (2015) to 66.8% (2022) — almost certainly a checklist artifact, not real language shift.

438 of 452 sucos are matched to a 2020-vintage boundary shapefile (no shapefile reflecting current suco boundaries exists publicly); 14 unmatched sucos, mostly in Dili, are not shown. Map colors for “dominant vernacular” exclude Tetun Prasa to recover a clearer picture of vernacular-language geography.

Markers in the homeland area mark known corrections from suco-office fieldwork records — click a marker for details. Click any suco (on the map or in the table below) to zoom to it.
